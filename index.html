MISSION OBJECTIVES / HOW TO PLAY
    CONTROLS: WASD MOVE · MOUSE AIM · LEFT CLICK FIRE · SHIFT SPRINT · C SLIDE · ESC PAUSE
    R9 / OPERATOR HANDBOOK

    FIELD MANUAL

    01 / BASE FLOOR: Fight 14 magenta hostile drones; the three with lime halos carry network fragments. Each drops a glowing green piece. Move close for a large E pickup prompt. Once you have all three, follow the dotted lime route on your minimap to the computer and press E to insert them.

    02 / SIGNAL FOUNDRY: Find three purple uplink towers. Stand inside a purple floor ring until it charges to 100%. Defend yourself during the hold. A secured tower and its ring both disappear. No E press needed.

    03 / SENTINEL ARENA: Defeat the giant moving mech. Watch its large red health bar at the top of the screen. It has heavy armor and calls in new drones. Destroying the boss is the only objective and unlocks the final achievement.

    Survival: Every mission begins with a brief objective overview. Hostiles move and fire energy missiles. Sprint, slide and use cover; health resets at each level start. If you die, retry that level. Radar: red = enemy, violet = objectives, lime dotted path = computer after all fragments are found.
